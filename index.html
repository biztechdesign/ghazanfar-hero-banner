Ghazanfar Bank · Hero background

A khatam lattice for the empty half

The hero on ghazanfarbank.com is a khaki gradient with nothing in the right two-thirds. aib.af fills that space with an oversized tone-on-tone motif revealed once on load — the technique, not the shape. The shape here is the khatam tessellation from your reference: eight-point stars on a square grid, tips touching, a small cross in every gap. Drawn as thin rounded outlines a few percent off the background, it stays a texture rather than a picture. All three are plain SVG and CSS — AIB ships a Lottie, this needs no library at all.

  • Grid 164px
  • Star tips touching
  • Stroke 1.5px at 55% white
  • Intro 2.9s, once
  • Clearing 0.62 × height
  • Payload ~5 KB, no library

Dropping it in

One component, no dependencies

HeroMotif.tsx is a client component. It goes between the gradient layer and the content layer of the hero section you already have.

The content layer already carries z-2, so the motif sits at z-[1] and never touches the headline.

Two masks, not one

A horizontal CSS mask fades the lattice out before it reaches the copy — transparent to 30%, solid past 66%. A second radial mask inside the SVG clears a circle for the mark, so the pattern never runs through it. On phones the first mask turns vertical at 72% opacity and the texture sits below the buttons.

Accessibility and cost

The layer is aria-hidden and pointer-events-none. Under prefers-reduced-motion every animation is dropped and the final frame renders immediately.

Tiles are generated to fill the measured hero, so no oversized off-screen grid is ever drawn.
